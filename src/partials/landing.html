THE TEAM

Four specialists. One workforce.

AUTONOMOUS WORKFORCE

Autonomous
from the first case.

Once your data sources are connected and Cortex starts opening cases, the workforce picks them up on its own. No agent playbooks to build and no prompts to write. You don’t start it. You check in on it.

ALREADY WORKING

Their queue. You oversee it.

This is the workforce’s own queue, live. You don’t work it case by case. You watch them work, teach them how your team does things, and approve the moves that need a human.
